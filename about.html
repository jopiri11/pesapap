About ChapaaExpress

Kenya's fastest-growing digital credit provider — bridging financial gaps with transparency, speed, and fairness.

Our Mission

To provide every Kenyan with instant, transparent, and affordable credit — empowering individuals to handle emergencies and seize opportunities without hidden fees or bureaucracy.

Our Vision

A Kenya where financial inclusion is universal, and every citizen has access to fair credit that builds their future rather than trapping them in debt.

Our Story

ChapaaExpress was founded in 2021 by a team of Kenyan fintech experts who saw a gap in the market: Kenyans needed fast, fair loans without predatory rates or complex paperwork. Traditional banks took days, while some digital lenders charged hidden fees.

We built ChapaaExpress to be different. 100% transparent interest rates. Instant M-Pesa disbursement. No origination fees. And a genuine commitment to helping our customers build positive credit history with CRB.

Today, we've served over 150,000+ Kenyans across all 47 counties, with a 98% customer satisfaction rate. We're proud to be a CBK-licensed Digital Credit Provider and a trusted partner for emergency loans, school fees, and business capital.
