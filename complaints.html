Complaint Resolution Timeline

Escalation Path

If your complaint is not resolved within 5 business days, you may escalate to:

  • CBK Complaints Hotline: 0800 22 55 66
  • CBK Email: complaints@centralbank.go.ke
  • ODPC (Data Privacy): info@odpc.go.ke
