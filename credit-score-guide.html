Understanding Your Credit Score in Kenya

A complete guide to CRB, credit scores, and how to build a healthy financial future.

Scores are calculated by TransUnion and Metropol CRB based on your repayment history, defaults, and credit utilization.

What is CRB and Why Does It Matter?

Credit Reference Bureaus (CRBs) in Kenya — TransUnion and Metropol — collect and maintain credit information from all licensed lenders, including banks, SACCOs, and digital lenders like ChapaaExpress.

Your CRB report is used by lenders to determine:

  • Whether to approve your loan application
  • How much you can borrow
  • The interest rate you'll pay
  • Loan repayment terms
Legal Note: Under Kenyan law, all licensed lenders must report both positive and negative credit information to CRBs. This means on-time repayments can actually improve your credit score!

How to Check Your Credit Report for Free

Kenyan law entitles you to one free credit report per year from each CRB. Here's how to access yours:

  1. TransUnion Kenya: Visit www.transunion.co.ke or dial *433#
  2. Metropol CRB: Visit www.metropol.co.ke or dial *433#
  3. Submit your ID number and phone number
  4. Verify your identity via OTP
  5. Download your credit report instantly

This will open the official CRB portal. ChapaaExpress does not store your credit report.

10 Ways to Improve Your Credit Score

  • Pay all loans on time — Even one late payment affects your score for months.
  • Repay early when possible — Early repayment shows financial discipline.
  • Avoid multiple loan applications — Each inquiry slightly lowers your score.
  • Keep credit utilization below 50% — Don't borrow your full limit.
  • Build a long credit history — Older accounts improve your score.
  • Dispute any errors on your report — Contact CRB directly for corrections.
  • Settle any existing defaults — Paid defaults still show but are better than unpaid.
  • Use different types of credit — Mix of mobile loans, bank loans, and SACCOs looks good.
  • Set repayment reminders — Avoid forgetting due dates.
  • Monitor your score regularly — Track progress every 3-6 months.

How Long Do Negative Listings Stay?

  • Late payments (30-90 days): 3 years
  • Defaults / Written off: 5 years
  • Bankruptcy: 5-7 years
  • Fraudulent activity: Indefinite
Good news: Positive information stays on your report indefinitely, helping you build a strong profile!

Common CRB Myths Debunked

  • Myth: "Paying a default removes it completely."
    Truth: It shows as "Paid in Full" but the default remains for 5 years.
  • Myth: "Only banks report to CRB."
    Truth: All licensed digital lenders, SACCOs, and even some utility companies report.
  • Myth: "Checking my score lowers it."
    Truth: Soft inquiries (personal checks) do NOT affect your score.
  • Myth: "A high score means I'll always get loans."
    Truth: Lenders also consider income, employment, and existing debt.
